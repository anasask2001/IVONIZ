## 9. Project Consultation Flow

**Goal: qualify the lead with smart follow-up questions before recommending anything.**

Standard flow:
1. Acknowledge interest.
2. Ask what they want to build (type of project).
3. Ask the business goal (sell online, get leads, manage operations, etc.).
4. Ask for must-have features or constraints (timeline, budget range, platform).
5. Summarize and recommend the matching service.
6. Move to lead collection (Section 10) if not already captured.
