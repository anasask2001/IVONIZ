## STAGE 2: Audience research

### What owners actually search (autocomplete, India + UAE)
- "why is my website not generating leads" · "website traffic but no sales" · "why am i not getting sales on my website"
- "why is my website not showing on google / not showing up when i google it / not indexed" (dozens of variants, incl. Wix, Shopify, WordPress, GoDaddy)
- "why is my google business profile not showing up / not showing on maps / not visible to customers / not showing after verification" (one of the largest clusters found)
- "do i really need a website for my business" · "can you run a business without a website" · "can you use instagram as a website" · "website vs instagram"
- "cheap vs expensive website" · "how much does it cost to maintain a website for a small business" · "how much website cost per month"
- "how long does seo take to work for a new website" · "how long does local seo take" · "seo vs google ads which is better for your business" · "local seo vs google ads"
- UAE: "is shopify good in uae" · "can we use shopify in uae" · "which payment method is best for shopify in uae" · "ecommerce website price in dubai" · "how to get leads in uae / dubai" · "ai automation uae" · "business automation dubai" · "how much does seo cost in dubai"
- Ecommerce: "reasons for cart abandonment" · "how to reduce cart abandonment on shopify" · "abandoned cart whatsapp message"

### Pain points behind those searches
1. **Paid for a site that sits there.** It looks fine, but nobody calls. Usually a conversion problem: unclear offer, weak service pages, no trust signals, hard-to-find WhatsApp/call.
2. **Can't be found.** Either the site isn't indexed, or the Google Business Profile isn't showing. Owners don't know these are two different problems.
3. **Developer disappeared / locked out.** Domain or hosting in the developer's name; nobody has the logins. Common with cheap freelancers.
4. **Cost confusion.** Quotes from ₹8,000 to ₹3 lakh for "a website"; nobody explains running costs.
5. **"Instagram is enough."** Many Kerala shops sell via Instagram/WhatsApp and question the website.
6. **SEO distrust.** Paid an agency, saw "rankings" reports but no enquiries; don't know how long SEO should take or whether ads are better.
7. **Gulf credibility.** UAE buyers check trade licence, address, reviews and payment security before trusting a site.
8. **Stores with visitors but no sales.** Slow mobile checkout, delivery costs revealed late, missing COD, weak product pages.
9. **Time drain.** Small teams answer the same WhatsApp questions all day and copy data between tools by hand (the automation and AI opportunity).

### Personas (short)
| Persona | Situation | Trigger to search | Biggest objection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kerala owner | Old or no website, WhatsApp-driven sales | Competitor shows up on Google; a customer asks "do you have a website?" | "Will it actually bring customers, or is it just a cost?" |
| Calicut / Malappuram local business | Clinic, showroom, retailer | Not appearing on Maps; old developer unreachable | "Can I trust this agency? What happens after launch?" |
| UAE SME | Crowded market, credibility matters | Losing deals to better-presented competitors; launching a store | "Will a team outside the UAE understand our market?" |
| GCC expander | Saudi / Qatar move | Needs Arabic, local payments, local trust signals | "Who handles Arabic and local rules?" |
| Startup founder | Idea, small budget | Needs an MVP quickly | "How much, how long, will it scale?" |
| Ecommerce owner | Store live, low sales | Ads cost rising, carts abandoned | "Is it the platform, the ads or the site?" |

### Lead-generation logic
- **Problem articles** (why no leads, not on Google, no sales) attract owners at the moment of frustration. CTA: a review of *their* site/store.
- **Decision articles** (cost, Shopify vs custom, SEO vs ads) attract owners choosing. CTA: a quote on the matching service page.
- **Each article links to one primary service page**, so authority flows to the pages that convert.
- **Search volume not verified** for most problem queries; autocomplete proves they're searched, not how often.
